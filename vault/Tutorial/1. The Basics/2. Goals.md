Secrets Within the Compound is a social deduction game for 6-16 [[Player|players]], and one [[Narrator]]. One person assumes the position of the [[Narrator]], whilst the other [[Player|players]] receive a secret [[role]] and a secret [[characteristic]] at the start of the game, this determines what team they’re on and grants them powerful game altering abilities. The [[Player|players]] are divided into two teams, the [[Loyalist]] team, and the [[Subversive]] team.
The Loyalists do not start the game knowing who the other Loyalists or Subversives are, so they must use a combination of logic and social skills to deduce who the Subversives are and eliminate them. The Loyalist team wins if there are no Subversive players left alive.
The Subversives must deceive the Loyalist players and remain hidden while they eliminate them. Unlike the Loyalists, the Subversives start the game knowing who their fellow Subversives are. The Subversive team wins if there is one or no Loyalist players left alive.
If the game results in a draw or stalemate, the Loyalist team wins
